The actor recipe

  • The actor is its data. No channels, no sockets as fields: the loop owns the runtime resources.
  • The loop consumes and returns Self. Inspect the final state, or run it again.
  • Spawning is the caller's call. Await it, spawn it, or put it in a FuturesUnordered.
  • No handle type. An associated function over the Sender keeps closed() and friends in reach — and hands back the receiver.
  • Natural shutdown. Drop the last sender; recv() drains, then yields None.
